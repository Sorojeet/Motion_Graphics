## Estimates and illustration
- The whole scenario is hypothetical, and is labeled on screen.
- The speed of leaf browning and fish deaths is illustrative. The leaf scene is labeled "Illustrative timing" and the oxygen bar "Illustrative meter".
- Real ethanol vapor is invisible. It is drawn in cyan and labeled.
- The flames are drawn brighter than real ones so viewers can see them, and this is labeled.
- Steam falling back as ordinary rain is simplified, and is labeled.
- The trees catching fire with orange flames and the camera feeling the heat first are dramatized.
- Fish are enlarged for legibility.

## Source quality
- The evaporation comparison with water relies on general chemistry: the cited sheet gives ethanol's rate, not water's.
- The leaf and poisoning claims use general references, not primary studies.
