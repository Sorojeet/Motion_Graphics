# Assets: What If You Became Immortal?

All 3D, sound and code assets are original and procedural, owned by the repository owner (see Assets/LICENSES.md).

- Reused: `Man` (jacket outfit), `Extra` (aging friends), `Camera`, `Sky`, `Instances`, `Glow` from the 3D kit; `RoundTable` and `Chair` from `gameprops.tsx`; theme colors and Montserrat and Inter fonts.
- New, added to Assets: `remotion/three/spaceprops.tsx` (Hourglass, Globe, Bubble, Earth, Sun, Candle, TimeCity, StarField), `remotion/components/counters.tsx`, `sfx/shimmer.wav`, `sfx/rumble.wav`, Noto Sans Devanagari and Arabic Bold fonts (OFL 1.1).
- Sound effects used: whoosh-short, whoosh-long, riser, impact, tick, heartbeat, pop, click, ding, explosion, ambience-drone, ambience-wind, fire-crackle, shimmer, rumble.
- Narration: Kokoro `am_michael`, 76.4 s, from `narration/scripts/immortal.txt`.
- Render: `render.sh OUTDIR [chrome path]`. The rendered video is not stored in the repository.
