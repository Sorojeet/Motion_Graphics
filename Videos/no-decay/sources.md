# Sources: What If Nothing Decayed?

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Land plants lock away about 60 billion tons of carbon a year (net primary production, about 60 Gt C/yr) | [IPCC, The Carbon Budget of Terrestrial Ecosystems](https://archive.ipcc.ch/ipccreports/sres/land_use/index.php?idp=24) |
| Atmospheric CO2 of 425.6 ppm (2025, preliminary); 1 ppm = 2.124 GtC | [Global Carbon Budget 2025](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/18/3211/2026/) |
| C3 plants survive down to about 150 ppm CO2 | [Swansong Biospheres II](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.4841) |
| Microbes decomposing organic matter release plant-available nitrogen; nitrogen-deficient plants turn yellow | [Crop Nutrition: Nitrogen](https://www.cropnutrition.com/nutrient-management/nitrogen/) |
| Soil carbon flux context (about 68 PgC/yr) | [Raich & Schlesinger 1992](https://data.ornldaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/public/global_soil/Respiration/comp/RAICH_abstract.htm) |
| Coal claim deliberately left out | [National Geographic on the 2016 PNAS study](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/getting-to-the-root-of-how-earths-massive-coal-seams-formed) |

## Estimates and illustration
- About ten years is our own simplified estimate: (425.6 - 150) ppm x 2.124 GtC/ppm = about 585 GtC, and 585 / 60 per year = about 9.8 years. It ignores oceans and nutrient limits, and is labeled on screen.
- The Year 1 and Year 5 markers and the pile depth are illustrative, not sourced.
- The fire scene is speculation and is labeled on screen.
